# PRD - Sucre Tourism Landing Page

## Project Overview
Create a modern, attractive, and informative landing page for the "Festival Nacional de Pito Atravesao" and local tourism in Sucre, Colombia. The page will serve as a digital showcase for the region's paraises, hotels, and cultural experiences.

## Target Audience
- National and international tourists.
- Culture and nature enthusiasts.
- Potential attendees for the Pito Atravesao Festival.

## Key Features
1. **Encuentro Intercluster Invitation**: A high-impact opening banner for the event on June 25-26, 2026.
2. **Infographic Layout**: A long, continuous scroll with angled section separators and dotted line pathways.
3. **Polaroid-Style Visuals**: Destination and hotel photos presented in white-bordered, slightly tilted frames.
4. **Accommodation Guide**: Curated lists of hotels in Coveñas and Sincelejo with direct contact info.
5. **Experiences Catalog**: MAPA TOURS, ECOTOUR ONNE, and MOMA with route details.
6. **Gastronomy & Cultural Spotlight**: Highlights of local flavors and the Pito Atravesao Festival.

## Design Identity: "Caribbean Infographic"
- **Style**: Mimics the Santander tourism reference with bold color blocks (Yellow, Turquoise, Palm Green, Red).
- **Elements**: Polaroid frames, diagonal transitions, and dotted line connections.
- **Colors**: Turquoise (#00B4D8), Palm Green (#2D6A4F), Sandy Beige (#F5F1E9).
- **Typography**: Montserrat (Headings) and Inter (Body).

## Technical Stack
- **Framework**: HTML/Vanilla JS or Vite/React (to be decided in implementation).
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS with custom properties for tokens.
- **Deployment**: Vercel.
- **Source Control**: GitHub.

## Success Criteria
- Responsive design (Mobile & Desktop).
- Performance score > 90 on Lighthouse.
- Visually stunning "WOW" factor.
- Seamless "One-Shot" implementation.
